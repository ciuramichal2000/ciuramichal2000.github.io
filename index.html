Invoice Hub

A complete accounts-payable web application: it reads invoices, imports bank statements, proposes which payment belongs to which invoice, and leaves every decision to a person.

  • Validates, does not guess: NIP and IBAN checksums, net + VAT = gross, due date after issue date.

  • Secure by design: hashed passwords, expiring tokens, roles enforced on the server, audit log, hostile text shown as text.
  • Tested end to end: 29 tests, five of them driving a real browser.

Reads invoices (text or PDF), imports a bank statement, and reports which invoices were paid, paid with a wrong amount, still unpaid, and which payments belong to no invoice. Has a command line and a REST API.
