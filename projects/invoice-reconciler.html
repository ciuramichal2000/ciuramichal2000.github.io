invoice-reconciler

Matches invoices to bank payments and reports what does not add up: paid, paid with a wrong amount, still unpaid, and payments that belong to no invoice.

The problem

In accounts payable the same question comes up every day: this invoice says 1,230.00 EUR, did the bank transfer arrive, and was it the same amount? Done by hand, over hundreds of documents, it is slow and easy to get wrong. This tool does that comparison and, more importantly, says plainly what it could not match.

How the matching works

  1. By reference. The invoice number appears in the payment description. Same amount: matched. Different amount: amount-mismatch, with the difference.
  2. By amount and date. No reference, but the same currency, the amount within a tolerance and a booking date 0 to 60 days after the issue date.
  3. A payment is used at most once. Whatever is left is reported as an unpaid invoice or an unmatched payment. Nothing is silently dropped.

Decisions worth mentioning

  • Never guess. An invoice without a number or a total is listed as skipped. A wrong guess would corrupt the reconciliation, and a skipped file is easy to look at.
  • Imports are idempotent. The same file imported twice changes nothing (unique keys in SQLite).
  • Amounts in every common format: 1,234.50, 1 234,50, 1.234,50, 1234.5.
  • The API has real error handling: 400 for malformed JSON, 404 for unknown paths, 413 for oversized bodies, 422 for content that cannot be read, all as JSON.
